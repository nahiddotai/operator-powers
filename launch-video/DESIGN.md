# Operator Powers launch video

60-second (58.5s) 16:9 launch film for X. 1920x1080, 60fps. No voiceover: kinetic type, product UI, music and sound design carry it, the same way ChatGPT and Codex launch spots do.

## Style prompt

Native ChatGPT. White and soft-gray product surfaces, near-black type, generous whitespace, soft shadows, rounded pills. Cinematic moments sit on OpenAI-style painterly gradient fields (sky blue, periwinkle, lilac, a little peach), with frosted-glass UI on top. The camera behaves like one continuous lens: fast eased push-ins onto the thing that matters, whip pans with motion blur, zoom-through cuts at peak velocity. Motion is smooth and long-tailed. Nothing bounces.

The reference cut (shapelayer's Codex spot) gave us the grammar: word-by-word blur-in type on white, a word morphing into a mic button, a hard push into the voice pill, a pan onto giant type with a colour wipe, a glass composer over a cinematic backdrop with an in-place word cycle, a giant zoom into the send button with a glow, floating product cards in depth, a small brand mark that drags text behind it, and a calm logo hold.

## Typography

Inter everywhere (closest open face to OpenAI Sans). Display: 500-600 weight, letter-spacing -0.035em. UI: 400-500 at native ChatGPT sizes (15px body, 14px sidebar) inside the window, which the camera enlarges.

## Logo

The OP monogram rebuilt as SVG from the shipped PNG (measured in 1024 space): O ring centre (416, 382), mid-radius 133, stroke 64; P stem x 531.5 from y 789 to 513.5, bowl centre (656, 594.25), mid-radius 80.75, stroke 60; tile `#030A1F`, corner radius 20%. Each glyph carries its own bottom-left to top-right gradient `#6155F3 → #A89BF5 → #EEEBFB`.

## What not to do

- No bouncy or elastic easing. `power3`/`expo` long-tail settles only.
- No OpenAI or Codex logos. ChatGPT and Codex appear as named surfaces and faithful UI, the brand mark on screen is always Operator Powers.
- No slow drifting push in the back half of a hold. Hold still or reveal the next thing.
- No lists of three in the copy, no "it's not X, it's Y" lines.
- No text smaller than readable once the camera settles.

## Storyboard (seconds)

Music: 120 BPM, 2s bars. Cuts land on bar lines.

### S1 Hook, 0.0-6.0, white
- 0.2 "Just describe the job" assembles word by word (blur 14 to 0, tinted lilac settling to ink).
- 1.3 "the job" dissolves into a round mic button; the line re-centres.
- 1.45 cursor glides in, clicks mic at 2.0 (ripple, soft blue fill).
- 2.05 camera slams in 3.4x on the mic; the button stretches into a voice pill with live waveform; a transcript tooltip types "There's a decision buried in this call. Find it."
- 3.05 whip pan right with motion blur onto giant "Your AI", grey-to-ink colour wipe.
- 4.05 cut to "finishes it." arriving oversized (inverse zoom-through), "it." in accent.
- 5.55 forward zoom-blur exit.

### S2 Product, 6.0-10.0, white with lilac glow
- 6.05 OP tile arrives out of the zoom, ripple rings; O then P draw themselves.
- 6.9 "Operator Powers" wordmark; 7.5 "28 AI skills for people who don't code."
- 8.6 subline cycles to "Now in ChatGPT and Codex."
- 9.55 zoom-through into the desktop.

### S3 Install in ChatGPT, 10.0-19.5, Sky gradient + ChatGPT desktop window
- 10.0 window arrives tilted in 3D and settles flat.
- 11.0 cursor to Plugins, click 11.8; plugin directory slides in.
- 12.5 click search, types "Operator"; result card slides in.
- 13.8 open the plugin page: icon, name, developer, tagline, description, Skills/MCP/Hooks, Try asking, Information.
- 15.0 giant push onto Install; click 15.75, glow, spinner, "Installed" with green check at 16.4.
- 16.5 pull back: toast "28 skills ready", Operator Powers appears in the sidebar.
- 17.4 back to "What can I help with?", composer carries the Operator Powers chip, audio file attaches, prompt types, send at 19.3.

### S4 Outcomes, 19.5-34.0, same window, depth-of-field on the result
Left: label + two-line outcome headline (waterfall cut between vignettes). Right: the conversation, camera locked on the result.
- V1 19.5 Meeting Miner: "A 90-minute call, down to three decisions."
- V2 23.2 Content Repurposing: "One episode, a whole week of posts."
- V3 26.8 LLM Council: "Pressure-test the call before you make it."
- V4 30.4 Customer Insight Synthesizer: "Twelve interviews, the patterns that matter."

### S4b Montage, 34.0-38.0
Finished deliverables float in depth, camera dollies through them; "28 powers" counts up, then "One install."

### S5 Codex, 38.0-44.4, Dusk gradient
- Glass composer "What should we work on?", label "Also in Codex".
- Types "Turn my notes into a launch plan", word cycles to "case study", "lead magnet".
- Pan and giant push onto send, click with glow, white flash.
- Three task cards run and complete: "Kick it off. Come back to finished work."

### S6 iPhone, 43.8-50.0, Morning gradient (overlaps S5 for the layered hand-off)
- ChatGPT iOS app rises in 3D over the blurred desktop; headline "All of it, right from your phone."
- Voice tap, transcript "Plan my week from these notes.", Weekly Review result builds.

### S7 Promise, 50.0-54.0, white
- The OP mark ripples and drags "No code to write." out behind it, erases it, then "No prompts to memorize."
- The mark grows into the end-card icon.

### S8 End card, 54.0-58.5, Apple style
- Icon, "Operator Powers", "Out now." Still frame to the end.
